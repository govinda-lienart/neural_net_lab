The loss bowl — watch a weight roll downhill

The curve is the loss (how wrong the model is) for one weight's value. The ball is the weight. Each step, we measure the slope (gradient) and step downhill.

Start point: weight sits up the side of the bowl → big error. Hit ▶ Play and watch it roll down.

Try this 🧪

1. Press Play with the default rate — smooth roll to the bottom.
2. Now crank the learning rate to 0.9+ and reset — watch it overshoot and bounce (too-big steps). This is why learning rate matters.
3. Set it very low (0.02) — it crawls, needs many steps. Too small = slow.

Press Fire a crop. The fingerprint lights up, flows along the wires, and the 5 scores appear. Highest score = the guess.

What you're seeing 👀

• Every one of the 6 inputs connects to every one of the 5 outputs → 6 × 5 = 30 wires = 30 weights (your real head: 384 × 5 ≈ 1,920).
• Forward: each output = sum of (input × wire weight). Highest wins = the guess.
• Backprop: if the guess is wrong, wires leading to the answer get "blamed" — they glow by how much they'd change. That glow is the gradient per weight.

The dimension ladder 🪜

0-D scalar — one number -1.33
1-D vector — a row [..]
2-D matrix — one grid 224×224
3-D tensor — your crop (3,224,224)
4-D tensor — a batch (32,3,224,224)
A tensor is just the umbrella word for a grid of numbers with any number of dimensions. Add a dimension → same thing, one level up.
